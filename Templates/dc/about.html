The Community Cloud

The AARI Data Center Bootcamp prepares Atlanta-area non-traditional learners for careers in modern data-center operations — from network security and facilities to remote hands and systems monitoring. Cohort members work asynchronously through weekly video modules while staying connected through division channels and cohort-wide discussion.

The Cohort System

You are placed in a regional cohort that moves through the program together. Each cohort has a general channel for announcements and community, plus division-specific channels where you collaborate with peers on your track. Instructors and admins review your hands-on lab submissions and provide written feedback.
